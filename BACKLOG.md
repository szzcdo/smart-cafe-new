CAFE-01 Register Account [MUST] - Customer can register with email and password; shows error if blank or duplicate.

CAFE-02 Login [MUST] - Customer can login and reach menu; invalid login stays on login screen.

CAFE-03 View and Search Menu [MUST] - Customer can browse drinks and cakes and filter by name or category.

CAFE-04 Manage Cart [MUST] - Customer can add, change quantity and remove items with live total price.

CAFE-05 Place Order and View History [MUST] - Customer can checkout, save order to Room and view past/current orders.

CAFE-06 Logout [MUST] - Customer can sign out and session is cleared.

CAFE-07 Order Notifications [SHOULD] - Simulated admin timer updates order status and sends notification.

CAFE-08 Ratings and Feedback [SHOULD] - Customer can leave 1-5 star rating and comment on orders.

CAFE-09 Feedback Sentiment Analysis [SHOULD] - Local Naive Bayes model tags review text as positive or negative.
